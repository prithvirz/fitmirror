How it works

Pick a garment from a shopping link or a photo, add your own photo, and an open-source AI model (IDM-VTON) draws the outfit on you. Most results take under two minutes.

Privacy

There is no account and no server of ours. Your photos go straight from your browser to the Hugging Face AI service and are not stored by IsoSmart. Your optional token stays in your browser.

About IsoSmart

IsoSmart is a solo-founder project from Jamshedpur, India, building free AI web apps. FitMirror is the first product. Early stage, no revenue yet.

Results are AI-generated visualisations, not exact fit previews. Complex patterns, dresses and side poses can show artefacts; retry with a different photo.
